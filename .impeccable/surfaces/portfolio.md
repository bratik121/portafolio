# Portfolio surface

## Scope

Single-page portfolio (`/`). Visitor mode: Experience. Language: Spanish and English. Light and dark.

## Direction contract

THESIS: A Swiss editorial portfolio with a pulse, refusing tiny centered stacks and fade-in-everything section entrances. The work and the name carry the page; motion is one authored gesture.

OWN-WORLD: Fixed palette of white/near-black grounds, deep red or warm orange as the single accent, warm greys for secondary text. One grotesque family at poster scale. Hairline rules, a 12-column grid, deliberate column offsets, and an arch silhouette repeated across portraits, project images and contact links. No gradients, no glass cards, no shadows standing in for hierarchy.

STORY: A visitor sees Bryant's name assemble at poster scale, reads his role and portrait immediately, then scrolls through evidence of skill and taste in a calm rhythm. Project evidence is always readable; contact is one decision away.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Name split across two lines in columns 1-8 at up to 6rem, role below a short accent rule, two actions; arched portrait in columns 9-12 with an offset accent arch behind it; vertical social links at the far left on desktop.

FORM: Code-led. `seed: none (pinned)` — "minimalismo editorial suizo, diseño de cuadrícula offset, espacio en blanco generoso, tipografía como protagonista absoluta" combined with "minimalismo extremo + detalle editorial, microinteracciones precisas".

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
